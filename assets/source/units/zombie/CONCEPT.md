# Zombie

## Character concept
A decaying humanoid wearing the remains
of ordinary medieval clothing.

## Visual identity
- Pale grey-green skin
- Cloudy eyes
- Thin, tangled dark hair
- Ragged brown clothing
- Torn green outer garment

## Backstory
Optional. Not yet established.

## Design notes
Should look like an ordinary, somewhat
pathetic undead creature rather than
a powerful monster.
